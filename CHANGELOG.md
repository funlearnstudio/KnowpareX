# Changelog

## 1.7.0

- Rewrote and consistency-checked 204 targeted junior-high and high-school mathematics and science curriculum units: high-school physics and chemistry; junior-high physical science; junior- and high-school mathematics, biology, and earth science.
- Improved curriculum search ordering so exact and title-related results rank ahead of incidental matches in long lesson text.
- Added semantic consistency checks and regressions for cross-subject contamination, formulas, terminology, examples, common misconceptions, and stage-appropriate content.
- Preserved the existing curriculum schema, adapter API, CLI commands, and total catalog size of 816 units.

This release does not represent a full rewrite of all 816 units. Elementary-school, language, social-studies, and other non-target curriculum units remain outside this rewrite scope. Automated audits and tests are consistency checks; they are not formal review or approval by subject teachers or other domain experts.
